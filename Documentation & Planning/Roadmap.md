# Roadmap — SoundChex for Roku

BrightScript/SceneGraph channel against the SoundChex API.

## Phase 1 — Channel + auth (R-01, R-02)
Sideloadable SceneGraph channel; profile picker + token auth; browse grids.
**Done when:** you can sign in and browse the library on a Roku device.

## Phase 2 — Playback (R-03)
`roVideoPlayer` streaming with resume + progress reporting.
**Done when:** video and music play with resume.

## Phase 3 — Publish (R-04)
Developer channel, then Roku Channel Store certification.

## Later
Roku Search / deep linking, voice, "continue watching".
